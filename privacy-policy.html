Vantwick ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

1What we collect

  • From you when you request a 5-day count: your business phone number, and optionally your first name and email.
  • When you become a customer: your business name and registration details (such as your EIN), which we need to register your texting with carriers.
  • Through the service, on your behalf: the phone numbers of people who call your business, call times, and the text messages exchanged with them.
  • A record of your consent to be contacted (the wording you agreed to and the time).
  • Basic technical data, such as IP address and browser type, from our website hosting.

2How we use it

We use your information to run the 5-day count, set up and run the text-back, send you results and inquiry notifications, bill you, and answer support questions. We use callers' information only to provide the service to your business. We don't use it for our own marketing.

3Texts and calls

By submitting the form, you agree that we may text, call, or email you about your 5-day count and your service. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-message opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party.

4Sharing

We don't sell or rent personal information. We share it only with providers that help us run the service, such as phone and texting platforms, form handling, and payment processing, and only as needed.

5Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system is completely secure. We keep information as long as we need it to provide the service and meet legal obligations, then delete it.

6Cookies

Our website doesn't currently use advertising cookies. If that changes, we'll update this page.

7Your choices

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your information. Email info@vantwick.com and we'll respond within a reasonable time. Callers who want their information removed can text STOP or email us.

8Children

Our service is for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18.

9Changes

We'll post changes here and update the date above.

10Contact

Questions about this policy: info@vantwick.com
