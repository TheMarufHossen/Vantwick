1What we do

We provide missed-call tracking and automated text-back for plumbing and HVAC businesses. This includes call tracking and counting, an automated text to people whose calls you miss, inquiry notifications, and reporting.

We don't answer calls and we don't book jobs. You handle the booking. The service doesn't replace your phone system, staff, or business operations.

2How it works

  • The 5-day count. You forward missed calls to us for 5 days (extendable to 7 if call volume is low). We only count missed calls. No texts go to your customers. At the end you get the number of missed calls and what they could be worth based on your average job.
  • Turning on text-back. Only if you choose to proceed. Before that, we'll ask for business registration details so we can register your texting.
  • Billing. Described below.

3Pricing: you pay only after results

You pay $297 per month only after we send you 5 qualified customer inquiries. If fewer than 5 qualified inquiries come in, you owe $0.

A qualified customer inquiry is a new customer in your service area with a real plumbing or HVAC need who replies to the text or leaves a callback number. Existing customers, spam, wrong numbers, out-of-area inquiries, and sales solicitations don't count.

Only inquiries that come in after the text-back is turned on count toward the 5. The 5-day count doesn't count toward the 5.

Billing starts when the fifth qualified inquiry is recorded. We'll tell you when that happens and send you a payment link.

4Disputes

We'll share the list of inquiries recorded by the system. If you think one doesn't qualify, tell us within 7 days of receiving the list. We'll review it with you and reply in writing.
